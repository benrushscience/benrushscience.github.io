# Project editing guidance

## CV maintenance

- Keep the CV page polished, current, and free of duplicate information.
- Before adding a manuscript or other CV information, check `data/cv.yml`, `pages/cv/index.html`, and the CV build script for an existing entry, including alternate titles and DOI matches.
- If requested information is already present, point that out to Ben and recommend updating or consolidating the existing entry instead of adding another listing.
- Do not add manuscripts that are in review.
- Maintain a single citation for each manuscript within the CV. A corresponding publication card on the Science page is a separate presentation of the same work.
- Edit `data/cv.yml` as the CV source of truth and keep the generated CV content in `pages/cv/index.html` synchronized.
- Preserve the established citation style: bold Ben's name, italicize the journal, and include a View DOI link when available.
