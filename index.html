Scientist, improviser, and teacher building ideas that connect with people.

Ben Rush brings together radiology data science, science communication, comedy, and facilitation to help audiences think clearly, collaborate bravely, and stay curious.

By day, Ben is a data scientist at the University of Wisconsin–Madison working in radiology and medical imaging. By night, he teaches improv, leads Amalgam Improv, and builds playful experiences that make complex ideas feel human.
